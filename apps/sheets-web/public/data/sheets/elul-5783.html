Elul 5783 - א

Source Sheet by David Nichol

Questions for thought

  1. What do these three passages have in common? Is there overlap in their goals, or the reasons/motivations they give, or the result they imply?
  2. How do the passages differ in those same respects?
  3. Which text do you find the most compelling? Why? See if your havruta partner chooses the same text or a different one. How do your reasons compare?
  4. Make a list of the ways these intersect with your life as it is now. Are you challenged to improve in some areas? Encouraged that you do certain things well? Inspired to try something different? List them in order of importance and discuss with your havruta.

Questions for thought

  1. This verse lists forty-eight ways of acquiring Torah. Which are your favorite of these, that you find the most compelling?
  2. Which do you find the least compelling, or even disagree with? Are there any you don't understand?
  3. Take your top two or three from each of the lists above, and discuss with your havruta partner. Did you pick any of the same ones? Why did you choose the ones you did? What does that tell you about yourself? As you talk about them, do you find yourself changing your mind?
  4. Give some thought to the month of Elul, a time of self-reflection and preparation. Do you have any goals for your own growth during this time? If not, can you find any in this list?
