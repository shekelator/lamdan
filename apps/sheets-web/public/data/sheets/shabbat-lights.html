Shabbat Lights

Intention: Move from weekday urgency to Shabbat presence.

Source

"The soul is granted an extra measure on Shabbat." (Talmud, Beitzah 16a)

"נר שבת חובה" - Lighting Shabbat candles is an obligation that creates peace in the home.

Practice Steps

  1. Pause for one full breath before lighting.
  2. Offer a personal intention for rest and connection.
  3. Notice one way your home feels different after the lights are lit.
