# Justice and Mercy

## Opening Prompt
How should a community hold accountability and compassion at the same time?

## Core Sources

> "Justice, justice shall you pursue." (Devarim 16:20)

> "עולם חסד יבנה" - "The world is built through kindness." (Tehillim 89:3)

Rambam teaches that a judge must search for truth while also preserving the dignity of every person standing before the court.

## Discussion
- Where does strict justice protect the vulnerable?
- When does mercy make justice possible?
- What practices keep leaders from becoming cruel or weak?
